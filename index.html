Discover the Brand

CALOTTÉ exists at the intersection of contemporary streetwear and considered design. Built around expressive silhouettes, restrained detailing, and a distinctive black-and-gold visual language, the label approaches everyday clothing with an editorial point of view.

Our focus is on the relationship between fit, texture, proportion, and detail. Every collection is an opportunity to explore a more considered approach to modern dressing.

CALOTTÉ is designed for those who value individuality without excess.
